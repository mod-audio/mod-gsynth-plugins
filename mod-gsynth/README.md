# mod-gsynth

A monophonic guitar synthesizer. Plug in a guitar or bass and play single notes: the pitch is tracked and drives a synth voice (oscillator, resonant low-pass filter, amplifier), while your picking dynamics open the filter and shape the volume. A plate reverb sits at the end.

The original signal is not mixed into the output. Play one note at a time, and turn Sensitivity down if the synth reacts to string noise or up if soft notes are missed.

Sweep opens the filter slowly while it is on and closes it again when it is off, taking Sweep Time each way. It is made for a footswitch.

Based on MOD Audio's "GSYNTH:Minimal" pedalboard, with the same chain and settings inside: pitch tracking from the DISTRHO PitchTracking Series and aubio, the oscillator, filter and amplifier from AMS, and the CAPS plate reverb.

LV2 plugin, `https://mod.audio/plugins/gsynth`. One audio input, stereo output.

## Where it comes from

Generated by MOD Audio's mod-gsynth-creator from the pedalboard **GSYNTH:Minimal**, snapshot **Init**. The signal chain is that pedalboard's, in one plugin.
`src/engines/minimal.h` draws the signal chain and holds its fixed values; `src/synth_config.h` holds this synth's
voicing and controls. The blocks in `src/blocks/` are ports of the plugins MOD's GSYNTH pedalboards are built from,
plus a few written for this project:

| Block | Original authors | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AMS VCO3, VCF, VCA and LFO2 | Matthias Nagorni, Aurelien Leblond (ams-lv2) | GPL-2.0 |
| CAPS Plate reverb | Tim Goetze | GPL-3.0-or-later |
| Invada Compressor | Fraser Stuart | GPL-2.0-or-later |
| MOD CV utilities | MOD Devices | GPL-2.0-or-later |
| MOD LowPassFilter | MOD Devices | GPL-2.0-or-later |
| AudioToCV Pitch, on aubio's yinfast | Bram Giesen, falkTX; Paul Brossier (aubio) | GPL-3.0-or-later |
| x42 fil4 high-pass and low-pass | Robin Gareus | GPL-2.0-or-later |

The combined work is **GPL-3.0-or-later** (`LICENSE`).

## Building

Needs the LV2 headers and single-precision FFTW (`fftw3f`).

```sh
cmake -S . -B build && cmake --build build
```

This tree is complete on its own. It was generated; to change the synth, change its spec and export again.
