# mod-gsynth-bassline

A monophonic bass synthesizer you play from a bass or a guitar. Play single notes: the pitch is tracked and drives a synth voice with a sub octave, a 24 dB resonant low-pass filter with its own envelope, glide, chorus and filter modulation. Mix blends your own sound back in.

Synth: Shape picks triangle, sawtooth or square. Voice adds two more oscillators an octave and an octave plus a fifth above. Sub Mix goes from the main oscillator to the one an octave below. Envelope sets how fast the filter envelope is, plucky to sweeping; fully down switches it off. Mod adds modulation.

Voice: how loud the added voices and the noise are, how far and which way the envelope moves the filter (below the middle it closes it, for a ducking effect), Glide, the amplifier's fade-in, and whether Mod is chorus or a filter wobble.

Setup: Compression goes from a level that follows your playing to every note the same. Set Tracking to your instrument: 5-string bass tracks down to a low B, 4-string bass is quicker and tracks down to a low E, Guitar is quicker still. Turn Sensitivity down if the synth reacts to string noise, up if soft notes are missed. Dry Thru FX sends your own sound through the filter, amplifier and chorus with the synth.

LV2 plugin, `https://mod.audio/plugins/gsynth-bassline`. One audio input, stereo output.

## Where it comes from

Generated by MOD Audio's mod-gsynth-creator. An original engine assembled from the block library, laid out like a monophonic bass-synth stompbox: three panels of controls, the first being the front panel.
`src/engines/bassline.h` draws the signal chain and holds its fixed values; `src/synth_config.h` holds this synth's
voicing and controls. The blocks in `src/blocks/` are ports of the plugins MOD's GSYNTH pedalboards are built from,
plus a few written for this project:

| Block | Original authors | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AMS VCO3, VCF, VCA and LFO2 | Matthias Nagorni, Aurelien Leblond (ams-lv2) | GPL-2.0 |
| Onset detector, attack/decay envelope, noise, chorus | MOD Audio, written for mod-gsynth-creator | GPL-3.0-or-later |
| Invada Compressor | Fraser Stuart | GPL-2.0-or-later |
| MOD CV utilities | MOD Devices | GPL-2.0-or-later |
| AudioToCV Pitch, on aubio's yinfast | Bram Giesen, falkTX; Paul Brossier (aubio) | GPL-3.0-or-later |
| x42 fil4 high-pass and low-pass | Robin Gareus | GPL-2.0-or-later |

The combined work is **GPL-3.0-or-later** (`LICENSE`).

## Building

Needs the LV2 headers and single-precision FFTW (`fftw3f`).

```sh
cmake -S . -B build && cmake --build build
```

This tree is complete on its own. It was generated; to change the synth, change its spec and export again.
