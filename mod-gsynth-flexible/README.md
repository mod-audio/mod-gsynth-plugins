# mod-gsynth-flexible

The full GSYNTH guitar synthesizer: every control of MOD's "GSYNTH:Flexible" pedalboard, in one plugin. Play single notes on a guitar or bass; the pitch is tracked and drives up to three oscillators, a filter and an amplifier, and your picking dynamics shape the sound. The original signal is not mixed into the output.

VCO: VCO 2+3 switches two more sawtooth oscillators in, tuned in octaves and semitones above the first. LFO>VCO is vibrato.

VCF: the cutoff follows the note (KEY>VCF), your picking (DYN>VCF) and an LFO (LFO>VCF). Filter > and Filter < sweep it up or down while held, taking Filter Time; they are made for footswitches.

VCA: FIX>VCA gives every note the same level, DYN>VCA follows your picking. Compress Dynamics evens the picking out before it is measured.

Turn Sensitivity down if the synth reacts to string noise, up if soft notes are missed.

LV2 plugin, `https://mod.audio/plugins/gsynth-flexible`. One audio input, stereo output.

## Where it comes from

Generated by MOD Audio's mod-gsynth-creator from the pedalboard **GSYNTH:Flexible**. The signal chain is that pedalboard's, in one plugin.
`src/engines/flexible.h` draws the signal chain and holds its fixed values; `src/synth_config.h` holds this synth's
voicing and controls. The blocks in `src/blocks/` are ports of the plugins MOD's GSYNTH pedalboards are built from,
plus a few written for this project:

| Block | Original authors | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AMS VCO3, VCF, VCA and LFO2 | Matthias Nagorni, Aurelien Leblond (ams-lv2) | GPL-2.0 |
| CAPS Plate reverb | Tim Goetze | GPL-3.0-or-later |
| Invada Compressor | Fraser Stuart | GPL-2.0-or-later |

| AudioToCV Pitch, on aubio's yinfast | Bram Giesen, falkTX; Paul Brossier (aubio) | GPL-3.0-or-later |
| SHIRO Modulay delay | Nino de Wit; genlib by Cycling '74 (MIT) | GPL-3.0 |
| x42 fil4 high-pass and low-pass | Robin Gareus | GPL-2.0-or-later |

The combined work is **GPL-3.0-or-later** (`LICENSE`).

## Building

Needs the LV2 headers and single-precision FFTW (`fftw3f`).

```sh
cmake -S . -B build && cmake --build build
```

This tree is complete on its own. It was generated; to change the synth, change its spec and export again.
